## Research Experience

* **Graduate Research Assistant, Prof. Vijay Gupta’s Lab**, Purdue University, Aug. 2024 – Present
  * Develop reliable perception and planning methods using conformal prediction, multi-sensor fusion, trajectory optimization, and safe reinforcement learning.
  * Developed conformal late fusion for dependent sensor outputs. Experiments on synthetic data, CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and MELD reduced prediction-set size by **10–25% at fixed coverage**.
  * Developed slack-inflated and source-tuned pseudo-calibration algorithms for domain adaptation, with Wasserstein-based coverage bounds under label-conditional covariate shift.
  * Developed single- and multi-agent deferred-decision trajectory optimization using mixed-integer conic optimization, conformal prediction, sequential candidate rejection, and receding-horizon planning.
  * Develop adaptive intrinsic–normal conformal prediction for structured responses on estimated low-dimensional manifolds, with fresh recalibration and set-convergence analysis under reconstruction and regression assumptions.
  * Developed barrier-continuation policy gradient (BCPG) for safe reinforcement learning with online parameter estimation and control barrier functions, evaluated in adaptive cruise control and planar navigation.
* **Research Assistant, Intelligent Networks Lab**, University of Tehran, Oct. 2023 – July 2024
  * Implemented deep deterministic policy gradient (DDPG) policies for networked epidemic control and budget-constrained opinion shaping.
* **Research Assistant, Human & Robot Interaction Laboratory (TaarLab)**, University of Tehran, May 2021 – Oct. 2022
  * Developed deep convolutional neural networks for person re-identification, attribute recognition, and attribute retrieval.
  * Built PyTorch pipelines with YOLOv5 and OSNet for detection, re-identification, and multi-object tracking.
  * Created CA-Duke labels for 32,000+ images and contributed to the SIVD dataset with 36,000+ images and 29 classes.
