## Research & Teaching Experience
* **Graduate Research Assistant, Prof. Vijay Gupta's Lab**, Purdue University, Aug. 2024 – Present
  * Develop reliable perception and planning methods using conformal prediction, multi-sensor fusion, trajectory optimization, and safe reinforcement learning.
  * Developed conformal late fusion for dependent sensor outputs, reducing prediction-set size by **10–25% at fixed coverage** on synthetic, CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and MELD benchmarks.
  * Developed slack-inflated and source-tuned pseudo-calibration algorithms for domain adaptation, with Wasserstein-based coverage bounds under label-conditional covariate shift.
  * Developed single- and multi-agent deferred-decision trajectory optimization using mixed-integer conic optimization, conformal prediction, sequential candidate rejection, and receding-horizon planning.
